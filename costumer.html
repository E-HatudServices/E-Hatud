Passenger Log In

Log in or create an account to book, sugo, and report.

  1. 1 Log In or Create Account: Sign in with your mobile number and password/PIN to access booking and sugo.
  2. 2 Choose Ride or Sugo: Select Ride for passenger trips, or Sugo for store errands and deliveries.
  3. 3 Lock GPS & Set Barangay: Tap Use My GPS, select your barangay, and enter a clear landmark or purok note.
  4. 4 Track Live & Settle Fare: Monitor your assigned rider and live meter on the map, then pay upon arrival.
  • Meter Starts on Pick-Up: Fare calculation begins only when the rider taps Pick Up (base fare + distance + running time).
  • Cancellation Policy: Free cancellation while still finding a rider; once assigned, call your rider directly.
  • Accurate Pin & Notes: Provide your exact GPS pin, active mobile number, and clear landmark notes to avoid delays.
  • Respectful Conduct: Treat riders and store staff courteously, and report any issue via Settings > Feedback.
